Welcome

This tool helps Kingshot players and alliance leadership prepare for Kingdom vs Kingdom.

Players can submit their available resources and speedups, allowing leadership to better coordinate and prepare for KvK.

How does it work?

1️⃣

Your leadership creates an event

Your alliance leadership creates a KvK event and shares the link with the players.

2️⃣

Players submit their information

Enter your player information, resources and available speedups.

3️⃣

Leadership coordinates KvK

Leadership can use the collected information to coordinate the alliance during KvK.
